About HTML Formatter

HTML Formatter is a free online tool that helps you organize and improve the readability of HTML code. Whether you are learning web development or working on a website, you can quickly format and manage your HTML without installing additional software.

Features of HTML Formatter

  • Format HTML: Organize HTML elements with proper indentation and spacing.
  • Validate HTML: Check for common structural and syntax issues in your HTML code.
  • Minify HTML: Reduce unnecessary whitespace and comments to make HTML more compact.
  • Custom Indentation: Choose between 2 spaces, 4 spaces or tabs.
  • Copy and Download: Copy your processed HTML or download it as a file.
  • Free and Browser-Based: Process your HTML directly in your browser without creating an account.
