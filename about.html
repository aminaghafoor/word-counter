About Word Counter

A simple online word counter designed to help you understand, measure and manage your writing. Check your word count, analyse your text and use practical tools in one place.

A Practical Tool for Everyday Writing

Word Counter is a free online writing tool that helps users measure the length of their text and review useful writing statistics. Whether you are preparing an assignment, writing an article, drafting a blog post or checking a document, the tool provides a convenient way to understand your text.

Instead of manually counting words or characters, you can enter or paste your text into the editor and see the statistics update as you write. The interface brings common writing measurements and text-management actions together in one straightforward workspace.

What Can You Do With Word Counter?

The tool provides several measurements that can help you review your writing:

  • Word count: Check the total number of words in your text.
  • Character count: View the number of characters, including spaces.
  • Characters without spaces: Check the character count after whitespace is excluded.
  • Sentence and paragraph count: Review the structure and length of your writing.
  • Line count: See how many lines your text contains.
  • Average word length: View the average number of characters per word.
  • Reading and speaking time: Get estimated times based on the length of your text.
  • Word goal tracking: Set a writing target and monitor your progress.

These statistics are intended to provide a useful overview. Sentence detection, word boundaries and time estimates can vary depending on the language, punctuation and writing style.

Import and Work With Documents

You can also use supported document formats instead of copying and pasting text manually. Word Counter supports importing TXT, DOCX, PDF and PPTX files so that their extractable text can be analysed in the editor.

After importing a supported file, you can review its text, check the statistics, copy the content or download text from the editor. Document import focuses on extracting text; it does not reproduce the original document's complete formatting or layout.

How Does Text Processing Work?

Text entered into the editor and supported files selected for import are processed in your web browser. The tool uses browser-based JavaScript to analyse text and extract content from supported documents. It does not require you to create an account to use the counter.

The text and selected document files are not automatically uploaded to our application server for counting. However, the website may load third-party libraries from their providers to support document processing. For more details about data handling, please read our Privacy Policy.

Who Can Use This Word Counter?

Word Counter is designed for people who regularly work with written content, including:

  • Students: Check assignment, essay and report word limits.
  • Teachers: Review the length of learning materials and written work.
  • Writers and bloggers: Monitor article length and writing progress.
  • Content creators: Check text before preparing or publishing content.
  • Everyday users: Count words and characters in general writing.

Our Approach

Our aim is to make everyday text analysis straightforward. Word Counter brings useful writing statistics and basic text-management features into a clean, accessible interface. The tool is designed to be convenient to use without unnecessary steps or account registration.

We aim to keep the experience practical, understandable and useful across desktop, tablet and mobile devices.
