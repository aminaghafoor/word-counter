Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Word Counter handles information when you use our online word counting and text analysis tools.

Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Word Counter. We provide a free online tool that helps users count words, measure characters and analyse text. This Privacy Policy describes how information may be handled when you visit or use this website.

Our current tool is designed to process the text you enter and supported documents you select directly in your web browser. You do not need to create an account to use the word counter.

2. Text You Enter Into the Tool

When you type or paste text into the Word Counter editor, the website uses browser-based JavaScript to calculate word counts, character counts, sentences, paragraphs, lines and other writing statistics.

The text entered into the editor is processed locally in your browser. The current application does not send that text to our application server for counting or store it in a website database.

Your text is not automatically saved by the word counter. If you refresh or close the page, the text in the editor may be lost. You should download or copy anything you want to keep.

3. Imported Documents and Files

Word Counter currently supports importing TXT, DOCX, PDF and PPTX files. When you select a supported file, your browser reads the file and extracts available text for analysis.

The selected document is processed in your browser rather than uploaded to our application server. The tool does not maintain a server-side copy of the document.

Document extraction may not preserve the original formatting, layout, images, charts or other visual elements. Scanned PDFs that contain images rather than selectable text may not produce extractable text.

You should avoid importing sensitive documents on devices or browsers that you do not trust.

4. Third-Party Libraries and Services

Some features rely on JavaScript libraries provided through third-party content delivery networks (CDNs). These libraries help the website read and process supported document formats.

The current implementation uses libraries including:

  • Grapheme Splitter: Helps count characters more appropriately for certain Unicode text.
  • Mammoth.js: Extracts text from DOCX documents.
  • JSZip: Helps read the contents of PPTX files.
  • PDF.js: Extracts text from PDF documents.

These libraries are loaded from external providers. When your browser requests a library, the relevant provider may receive technical information associated with the request, such as your IP address, browser information and request details, according to its own practices and policies.

Loading a library from a CDN does not mean that the text you enter or the documents you import are automatically sent to that CDN. The libraries are used by your browser to process the selected content.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

The current version of Word Counter does not require cookies, user accounts or a database to perform its main text-counting functions.

The website uses browser Local Storage to remember your selected light or dark theme. This preference is stored on your device using the browser's Local Storage feature.

Text entered into the editor and the contents of imported documents are not stored in Local Storage or Session Storage by the Word Counter application.

Third-party providers and the website's hosting infrastructure may use their own technologies or maintain technical logs. Their practices are governed by their respective policies.

6. Analytics and Advertising

At the time of this policy's last update, Google Analytics, Google AdSense and other website analytics or advertising services have not been added to the current project.

If analytics, advertising or other tracking services are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain the relevant services, information they may collect and available user choices.

7. Data Security and Retention

Since the current word-counting process takes place in your browser and does not require an application database, the website does not retain the text you enter as part of its normal counting process.

However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Your browser, device, internet provider, hosting provider and third-party services may process technical information as part of providing their services.

8. Children's Privacy

Word Counter is a general-purpose writing tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not require users to register or provide personal details to use the main counter.

Users should avoid entering personal or sensitive information into the editor when it is not necessary for their task.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when website features, technologies or data-handling practices change. Any revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically to stay informed about how the website operates.

10. Questions About This Policy

If contact details are published on this website in the future, you can use those details to submit questions about this Privacy Policy.

Important Information

This policy describes the current intended operation of Word Counter. It should be reviewed and updated whenever new services, tracking technologies, storage features or data-processing practices are introduced.
